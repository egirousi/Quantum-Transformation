Project Overview 🧑‍💻
This document explores the role and impact of quantum computing in the transportation sector. It provides an in-depth analysis of how quantum technology can optimize transport systems, improve efficiency, and reduce operational costs. The study is part of the "Digital Economy" course at the University of Macedonia, conducted by a team of Applied Informatics students under the supervision of Professor Emmanouil Steiakakis.
